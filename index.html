Reaction Simulator

Engineer+

Interactive CSTR/PFR reactor network simulator with Levenspiel plots, conversion & temperature profiles, thermal operating diagram, and dynamic response with disturbance injection. 07

QM Property Predictor

Engineer+

Does quantum chemistry actually improve molecular property prediction? Ran B3LYP/6-31G* on 200 molecules with PySCF and measured it properly — scaffold k-fold, paired bootstrap, and noise controls. Answer: no, once fingerprints and RDKit descriptors are in hand. The live predictor ships a TreeSHAP explanation and an applicability-domain warning with every prediction. PySCF · RDKit · XGBoost · PyTorch.
